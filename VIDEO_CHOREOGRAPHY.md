# 🏆 Agapita: 3-Minute Video Choreography & Script
**Target Duration:** 3:00 Minutes (180 Seconds)  
**Target Audiences:** Google Gemma 4 Good Hackathon & SEA CIC-SIC 2026 Judges  
**Vibe:** High-energy, professional, empathetic, and technologically sophisticated.

---

## 📋 Pre-Recording Setup Checklist
Before you press **Record**, make sure your local dev environment is in perfect, polished order:

1. **Laptop Setup:**
   * Open Chrome and load the client dashboard: `http://localhost:5173`.
   * Log in with a clean Caretaker Account to the dashboard homepage (`alerts` or `scanner` tab).
   * Ensure your PC speaker volume is turned up to **70%+** so your mic can clearly record the offline **Kokoro-82M TTS** spoken intents!
2. **Android Phone / iPad Setup:**
   * Ensure your phone is connected to the same local Wi-Fi.
   * Open Chrome on the phone and load: `http://172.20.10.3:5173`.
   * Log in as the patient/caregiver, navigate to the **Live AR Scanner**, and verify the camera feed is active and smooth.
3. **Environment Prep:**
   * Place 2–3 physical test items on your desk next to your keyboard (e.g., a **pill bottle**, a **water bottle**, or a **mug**).
   * Ensure your room has good lighting so the camera feed looks ultra-premium.

## ⏱️ The 3-Minute Storyboard & Choreography

### 🎬 Segment 1: The Hook & Social Impact
**Time:** `0:00 - 0:30` (30 Seconds)  
**Visual Action:**
* Start with a close-up screen recording of your laptop showing the **Agapita Caregiver Login page**. 
* Hover your cursor over the glassmorphic card, highlighting the text at the bottom: **`OFFLINE EDGE NODE: LOCAL FACILITY ONLY`**.
* Log in cleanly, showing the beautiful dashboard pop into view.

🎙️ **Voiceover Script:**
> *"Every single day, millions of professional and family caregivers face immense burnout. For patients with cognitive decline, dementia, or speech barriers, communicating basic physical intents becomes a daily struggle.*
>
> *Meet **Agapita**—a secure, 100% offline, local-first communication and ambient grounding bridge designed to give patients their voice back, while ensuring absolute medical privacy."*

---

### 📸 Segment 2: The Leica-Inspired AR Viewfinder
**Time:** `0:30 - 1:10` (40 Seconds)  
**Visual Action:**
* Switch the video angle to show your physical **Android Phone** or **iPad** held in your hand.
* Navigate to the **Live Scanner** view.
* Tap the floating camera icon to turn on the viewfinder.
* Demonstrate swapping the camera lenses using the **Lens Flip button** (swapping from front-selfie lens to back-environmental lens) to show the responsive hardware-accelerated transition.
* Use the slider to zoom in and out smoothly.

🎙️ **Voiceover Script:**
> *"At the heart of Agapita is our premium, high-fidelity AR ambient viewfinder. Inspired by professional Leica aesthetics, the interface is stripped of cluttered target guides, featuring a centered double-ring shutter and intuitive floating lenses.*
>
> *Under the hood, we engineered an active WebKit track-release yielding mechanism. This guarantees instant, freeze-free camera lens toggling and native hardware zoom on standard mobile browsers, completely offline."*

---

### 🧠 Segment 3: Multimodal AI Bounding & Grounding
**Time:** `1:10 - 1:55` (45 Seconds)  
**Visual Action:**
* Point your phone's camera at the **pill bottle** or **water cup** on your desk and tap the **Shutter Button**.
* Show the processing loading indicator spin for a brief moment.
* **Boom!** Bounding boxes render on the phone's screen.
* Zoom in to show the card: **Type:** `Medication` | **Name:** `Pill Bottle` | **Details:** `Resting next to the keyboard`.
* Tap the green **"Add Grounding Factor"** checkmark button.

🎙️ **Voiceover Script:**
> *"When a caregiver or patient snaps a scan, our edge-native intelligence takes over. Using a local **Gemma 4 Vision** model running privately on our facility server, Agapita detects clinical and environmental factors in real time.*
>
> *It isolates medications, dose states, and room layouts, dynamically drafting clinical sentences. With a single tap, the caregiver logs this ambient context directly into our offline patient database, creating an active clinical grounding profile."*

---

### 🔊 Segment 4: Real-Time Sync & Kokoro-82M Voice Synthesis
**Time:** `1:55 - 2:40` (45 Seconds)  
**Visual Action:**
* Switch camera view to show **both the Laptop and Phone side-by-side**.
* Trigger a patient intent request from the phone (e.g., tap a "Need Water" or "Assistance" card).
* Watch the notification card instantly slide onto the Laptop Caretaker Dashboard via WebSockets.
* **Sound Cue:** Let the laptop speakers play the highly natural, warm spoken audio from **Kokoro-82M**: *"Alert: Patient requires drinking water."*

🎙️ **Voiceover Script:**
> *"Communication is a two-way street. Whenever an intent is triggered, our local WebSocket layer synchronizes the data across all active facility devices in milliseconds.*
>
> *To confirm the patient's intent with maximum empathy, the server passes the text to **Kokoro-82M**—a state-of-the-art, 82-million parameter offline Text-to-Speech engine. It synthesizes studio-quality, warm human voice feedback instantly, completely offline."*

---

### 🏆 Segment 5: The Grand Close & Pitch
**Time:** `2:40 - 3:00` (20 Seconds)  
**Visual Action:**
* Show a beautiful, sweeping view of both devices syncing, then transition to your project's presentation slide or the closing logo screen.

🎙️ **Voiceover Script:**
> *"Agapita proves that the future of healthcare AI doesn't belong to expensive, centralized cloud monopolies. By democratizing open-weights models like Gemma 4 on simple consumer hardware, we protect patient dignity, secure clinical privacy, and support caregivers where it matters most—locally.*
>
> *Agapita: Secure. Assistive. Private. Thank you."*

## 💡 Pro-Tips to Maximize Your Submission Score:
* **The Telemetry Flex:** When showing the server startup logs or the scan completed screen, point out how fast the Gemma 4 Vision prefill and decode speeds are (under 2 seconds local!). Judges *love* concrete performance statistics.
* **Keep Hands in Frame:** When demonstrating the phone/iPad, physically interact with it in front of the camera. It proves the app is fully functional and responsive, not just a pre-rendered video.
* **Highlight the Network Purity:** Point out that during this entire demo, your laptop Wi-Fi had **no internet connection active**, demonstrating true edge capabilities.
